# MSH audit — 19 November 2026 — the screen's real estate

Owner instruction, 19 November 2026: *massacre spatial hallucinations and kill
all the AI design slop; the real estate economy for the screen is very
important; scrolling is always bad unless there is something really important
down there; make it work from an operational point of view.*

This audit measures the interface the reader actually sees, not the source. Two
instruments were written for it and are now part of the release gate:

- `tools/viewport/realestate.py` — per surface and viewport: how much of the
  screen carries content, how much is chrome, how tall the largest empty band
  is, and how many pages each flow needs.
- `tools/viewport/pagefill.py` — per reading page: how full it is, how tall its
  empty band is, **where** that band sits, and whether a block is split across
  pages.

The second point matters. An empty band under the content is a short page: the
flow has run out of material and no content may be invented to fill it. An
empty band *inside* the content is a hole — a page that asks the reader to turn
for nothing. A blank page is worse than both. The instrument now classifies the
band by position, and the release gate fails on a hole, a blank page, or a
page that is mostly empty and is not the end of its flow.

Baseline: the live release `40f7bbf`, measured with the same instrument that
passes the gate: 42 reading pages across the five surfaces (Explore, Prepare,
Drill, Research, DDPM) at 390×844, 1024×768 and 1440×900.

## Found

Each item names the element, the viewport and the task cost it imposed.

| # | Viewport | Surface | Page | What the reader got | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 390×844 | Explore | 4 of 7 | the route profile's title and distance line, alone | 30% fill, 112px void (66.8%) — a fragment page |
| 2 | 390×844 | Explore | 7 of 7 | nothing | 0% fill, 160px void (95.5%) — a blank page |
| 3 | 1440×900 | Explore | 2 of 2 | the choke list alone, inside a stretched container | 22.7% fill, 592px void (75.9%) |
| 4 | 390×844 | DDPM | 3 of 3 | the tail of the 22-row province table | 34.1% fill, 480px void (65.6%) |
| 5 | 1024×768 | Explore | 2 of 2 | the choke list alone, inside a stretched container | 74.1% fill, 144px void (22.2%) |
| 6 | 390×844 | Research | 9 of 9 | the last research block | 68.1% fill, 200px void (27.3%) — a short page, the end of the flow |

Six causes, found by reading the cascade in the browser rather than the source:

1. **A block taller than the page was forbidden to break.** `.terrain-secondary`
   carries `break-inside: avoid` on the phone, but the block is 390px tall and a
   phone reading page is 168px. The rule could not be honoured, so the browser
   pushed the overflow into a column of its own — the blank page 7.
2. **The page count was rounded up.** The columns are exact: N columns of width
   W with gap G measure `N*(W+G)-G`. The count used `ceil` on a scroll width
   that carried a 2px sub-pixel overflow, so six columns were counted as seven.
3. **A title could not travel with its chart.** `break-after: avoid` only
   applies to block-level boxes, and the route profile's title was an inline
   `<strong>`, so the rule had nothing to hold. The title and the chart were
   free to part company.
4. **The chart's key was a separate box.** The elevation profile's key sat in
   its own flow box after the drawing, so a break could leave the key on the
   next page, explaining a drawing the reader can no longer see.
5. **A container was stretched past its content.** The analysis column carried
   `flex: 1`, so it filled the 780px operations column with 357px of material
   and the remaining 423px read as an empty band under the content.
6. **The phone compaction of the chart never ran.** `.profile-chart` (four rule
   blocks) and `.queue-chart*` (nine rules) matched no element in the app. Three
   separate rules set the chart's height — 44px, 80px, 50px — and all three were
   defeated by `#main .terrain-operations .profilequeue svg { height: auto }`
   (specificity 1-2-1). The phone carried the same 108px chart the rules were
   written to shrink.

## Changed

No content was deleted, no spacer was added, and no colour was introduced.

- **`src/PagedFlow.tsx`** — the page count now rounds to the nearest column
  instead of up. A sub-pixel overflow no longer invents a blank page.
- **`src/viewport.css`**
  - the phone's analysis container may break between blocks;
  - the profile's title, distance line, chart and key stay together
    (`break-after: avoid` on the title and the live line, `break-inside: avoid`
    on the chart);
  - the profile's figures travel with the cue that explains them;
  - the analysis container is no longer stretched past its content
    (`flex: 0 0 auto`);
  - on a full-height column (≥761px) the route analysis is one block, so the
    profile and the choke list read together on one page;
  - on the phone the choke list is one line per point, so the whole list sits
    beside the profile's figures instead of three tall cards;
  - the phone's section gaps above the profile and the analysis row were
    reduced by 8px, which is what let the title share the chart's page;
  - the dead `.profile-chart`, `.profile-y-scale`, `.profile-x-scale` and
    `.queue-chart*` rules were deleted.
- **`src/charts.tsx`** — the profile chart's key is drawn inside the chart's own
  box, so a page break can never separate a drawing from its key.
- **`src/viz.css`** — the same key is positioned inside the chart's box; the
  phone's DDPM flow keeps the `break-before: column` on the source receipt and
  the province table, so a 22-row table no longer straddles a page boundary.
- **`src/style.css`** — the dead `.queue-chart*` rules and the chart height
  that no rule could reach were deleted.
- **`tools/viewport/pagefill.py`, `tools/viewport/realestate.py`** — the
  instruments, committed. The fill instrument classifies the empty band by
  position, waits for the layout to settle before it measures, records the
  bytes it measured against, and can fail a release.
- **`package.json`** — `npm run verify:pages` (the slop gate), `npm run
  verify:realestate`, and `npm run release`: test → build → contrast → the slop
  gate → deploy → fresh receipts → the receipt gates.

## Verified

Measured on the built bundle in Chromium at 390×844, 1024×768 and 1440×900
with `tools/viewport/pagefill.py`, and on the live release with the existing
gates.

**The phone Explore flow: 7 pages with a fragment and a blank page → 5 pages
with neither.**

| 3 | 75% | 75% | the time-to-the-end instrument and its note |
| 4 | **30% — the title alone, 112px void** | **100%** | the profile's title, live line, chart and key |

**Every other surface.**

| Surface | Before | After |

**The whole set:** 42 pages measured → 40; 1 blank page → 0; 1 fragment page →
0; 4 pages with a void over ⅓ of the slice → 0; 34 pages splitting a block →
30. The gate (`npm run verify:pages`) exits non-zero on a blank page, a hole or
a fragment, and its receipt records the bytes it measured against.

**A note on the instrument itself.** The first version of it read a page's
content as text elements only — it did not count `<span>` or `<strong>`. The
profile's figures and the choke card's heading are marked up as spans, so the
gauge reported the gap between two pieces of text as an empty band. The
interface was right and the gauge was wrong; the numbers above are from the
corrected instrument on both sides, and the old numbers were worse than they
looked.

**Also run on this build:** `npm test` — 91 TypeScript tests and 11 Python
tests pass; `node scripts/check-contrast.mjs` passes; the Results pagination
gate renders in Chromium and WebKit and passes.

## Remaining

Not fixed. Named, with the number that would close each one.

1. **1440 Explore, page 2: 46.4% fill, a 392px tail.** The operations column
   holds about 903px of content in an 820px window, so it needs two pages. The
   split is now designed — the instrument and the finding on one page, the
   route analysis on the other — instead of a chart split from its figures. To
   make it one page, about 83px of content would have to leave the column. The
   only candidate is the 115px intervention chart in the finding panel, which
   the Results surface already carries in full and which the phone already
   hides. It was left in place: the desktop keeps the evidence, and a page that
   is 46% full at the end of a flow is a short page, not a hole.
2. **1024 Explore, page 2: 71.6% fill.** The same column at 264px wide. Same
   arithmetic, same decision.
3. **The phone Explore column still carries seven blocks in 168px.** The
   profile's chart is 108px because the chart draws its own box; a shorter
   chart would be a squiggle, and the chart is the evidence.
4. **The 390 Research flow is nine pages**, and its last page is 68.1% full.
   The pages before it are 82–100% full. The last page is the end of the flow.
5. **The 390 DDPM flow's first two pages are 67% and 70% full.** Each of the
   source receipt and the province table now begins a page; a receipt that ends
