# Palette choice

Intent: let people orient themselves and compare the paper's measures without mistaking color for a hazard level.
Relationship: a cool, dark masthead and a sparse warm pointer above a neutral reading surface.
Chord: Palette Plate 002, Dark Tyrian Blue (#12354e) and Yellow Orange (#f99d1b, digital conversion); neutral paper and ink carry the work. Action amber is the house adaptation #f59e0b.
Roles: neutral ground and ink for reading, blue masthead as a bounded atmospheric field, amber for selection and action. No green/red risk coding.
Budget: neutral carriers dominate; masthead is bounded; amber remains sparse.
Risks: amber needs dark text on light action fills; amber body text on paper is avoided. A blue field never represents current hazard or safety.
Proof: contrast script in scripts/check-contrast.mjs; text labels and numeric values preserve meaning without hue. Responsive evidence recorded separately.

Source: https://github.com/Nonarkara/palette/tree/a419d274285843c166bde22e22cd4e11006b7ad3 ; https://colors.nonarkara.org/#plate-002 . Values are credited screen conversions, not exact historical printed inks. The source plate was checked in local data/colors.json. Third-party credit is in public/vendor/PALETTE-NOTICES.md.
