# MSH visual pass: charts in place of prose (2026-11-02)

The previous passes checked the source code for radius values and English-only strings. This pass checked the rendered screens. The data was already there, but most of it reached people as prose, small text tables and paging.

## What changed

- **Chart kit** (`src/charts.tsx`, `src/viz.ts`): small inline SVG charts, drawn in CSS pixels so text stays on the type tokens.
  - `StepDown`: the five cumulative packages, with the 30′ and 45′ warning conditions on one % scale. Each step can be selected.
  - `DotGrid`: 100 dots split into outcomes. It is drawn only when the paper reports all three outcomes; other packages show "not classified".
  - `StackBar`: a stacked bar whose parts always sum to the population.
  - `PaceRuler`: free-flow minutes for each pace, with a target line.
  - `ProfileQueue`: terrain profile and queue waits on one distance axis, with the walker's position as a playhead.
- **Model panel**:
  - The published finding sits at the top.
  - Controls are in a 4-column grid. The colliding labels ("Runners" over "No face", a wrapped "Water demo") are fixed.
  - The crowd counts are now one stacked bar directly under Start.
  - The 2×2 pace text and the unreadable 13-label queue strip are replaced by the ruler and the shared-axis chart.
- **Results**:
  - `StepDown` replaces the paired bars.
  - `DotGrid` replaces the outcome strip.
  - The layout is two columns at 1024 px and up; it was half empty.
  - On phone the pane now fits the screen. Before, it ran under the footer with no pager.
  - The "reduction" badge is no longer green, which the palette forbids.
- **Research**:
  - `StepDown` above the results table, which keeps every number.
  - The boxed "DDPM source audit" band is now an inline link.
  - Sources & sensors is folded in as four "○ not connected" rows (owner decision). The Satellite tab is gone, and `#satellite` redirects to `#research-sources`.
- **Drill**:
  - The five protocol stages form one numbered row, in reading order.
  - The headcount check is a counted / not-accounted bar.
- **DDPM**: provinces are ranked with bars, and risk codes are shown as one 100% bar.
- **Prepare**: the walking-time ruler compares three paces, and the checklist shows a count (n/6 ✓).
- **Type**:
  - About 22 px sizes collapsed to four tokens: 11 / 13 / 17 / 24.
  - Each view now uses 3–5 distinct rendered sizes; it was 5–11.
- **Shadows**: drop shadows are replaced by 1 px hairline rings, and glow filters are removed.
- **Pager** (`PagedFlow`):
  - It now re-counts pages when a chart resizes, and again at 1.5 s and 5 s.
  - Before this, late layout left the last page unreachable.
- **Phone map caption**: no longer tucked under the map.

## Independent audit

A second agent on a different model reviewed the screenshots against MSH §3. Its verdict was **REVISE**. What happened to each finding:

1. **Results chart labels out of line with their points at 1024 px.**
   - Cause: the chart measured its width while its pane was hidden.
   - Fixed: width is re-checked on every render.
2. **Half-empty Results page.**
   - Fixed: two columns, a 240 px chart and a 150 px dot grid.
   - Some empty space is still left below at 1440 px. The content is short, and adding filler would break the no-invented-content rule.
3. **Phone Start not on the first screen.**
   - Fixed: the finding chart is 44 px on phone and its footnote moved to the title attribute.
   - The map is about 51% of the 390 px screen. The owner's banner and caption rules set that.
4. **Pace target line crossing a label.**
   - Fixed: the target line is now a short tick.
5. **Bare "4:35" on the queue axis, and sliver bars.**
   - Fixed: the label now reads "max wait 4:35", and bars are at least 3 px wide.
6. **Amber/brown used on data.**
   - Queue, queued, missing, DDPM code 4, the run marker and "Key Finding" now use ink.
   - The victims dot keeps `--segment-victims`, because Palette Plate 002 (docs/PALETTE.md) assigns that colour to that role.
7. **Missing spaces in the research copy.** Fixed.
8. **Drill step text at 11 px.** Fixed: now 13 px.
9. **DDPM code segments hard to tell apart.** Code 4 is now ink. Every legend entry carries its number.
10. **An orphan paragraph at the 1024 px research column break.** Not fixed. It comes from the CSS-column paging, and changing it is out of scope.
11. **Prepare looks unbalanced.** Left as is; nothing was invented to fill the space.
