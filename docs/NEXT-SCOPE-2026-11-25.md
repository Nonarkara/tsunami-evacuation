## Scope and release rules

- Preserve interactive maps, charts, Results, Planner, Drill, Research, DDPM, the manual, street linework, local form data and imported replay state. Reuse Palette 002, Jost and Prompt, the 36 px banner, 42–44 px map entry, compact controls and the 60 px phone caption budget.
- Preserve sourced coordinates and explicit heights, the 1.65 m eye camera, queue accounting, published denominators, provenance and model time origins. Exercise calculations and imported research frames retain their own meanings. Operational wave forecasts, new beach coverage and approved refuges require a separate scope with validated inputs.
- The browser matrix covers English and Thai at 390×844, 1024×768 and 1440×900, plus enlarged reading text, 320×700, 390×600, 667×375, 1169×674, 1280×720 and both sides of the banner breakpoints. Measure substantive content and actions inside the visible frame. Include end-of-page, focus return and fallback states. Obtain independent visual review for changed surfaces.
- Run tests, strict production build, contrast and fresh browser checks before publishing each slice. After deployment, verify actual served bytes and the affected task on the live site. A physical phone pass covers touch, 1×/30× motion, visibility resume and Thai reading. Report unavailable physical-device or user checks separately from browser results.
- Recheck the branch and public baseline immediately after approval. Suggested effort is 5–8 focused working days across all five releases, subject to the first performance traces and access to physical devices. Release 1 can ship on its own. Review measured performance after release 2 before spending time on deeper renderer changes.

Approval authorizes the five releases in this order, including commit, push, deploy and live verification. The percentage targets are proposed engineering budgets and should be read against the measured baseline. A material scope change comes back for approval.
