# MSH audit and release record — 12 November 2026

Scope: the whole workspace at 390×844, 1024×768 and 1440×900, with the named targets
of the owner's instruction: text boxes, the bottom-bar toolbars, the pop-up bars and
anything carrying textual information. Every finding below was **measured on the
rendered pixels**, not read from the source.

Two instruments produced the evidence, both in the repository:

- `tools/viewport/bands.py` — reads the rendered lines of each text band: the exact
  characters on each line, the measure in characters per line, and whether the last
  line is a stranded word. It also records the chrome cost (the height of the fixed
  bands above and below the workspace and their share of the viewport).
- `scripts/verify-components.py` — drives a built site in Chromium and checks the
  components a release would be broken without: the terrain reaching a ready state,
  the water re-shading as a replay advances, the roster conserving its total, a drill
  record surviving a reload, the research index resolving, the data-and-sources dialog
  staying inside the viewport, the masthead being one row at seven widths, and the
  navigation symbol for every section.

The rule that decides all of it: **the measured interface is the interface.** A
source that reads well, a green build and a passing gate are not a pass. Two faults in
this session — a static wave ramp and a fourteen-character drill measure — passed
every gate and were found only by measuring the rendered page.

## Changed

1. **Stranded words.** A qualified term now breaks as a unit: "endpoint unapproved"
   and "(practice only)" are kept together by a nowrap span, so the second line carries
   a phrase instead of one stranded word. The route cue lost its duplicate scope
   sentence entirely, which put it back on one line at every width.

2. **One quantity, one name.** The crowd readout says **max wait**; the chart legend
   names the series it draws, **wait per segment**; the chart marker names that
   series' maximum, **max per segment**. Three names for three distinct quantities,
   instead of three names for one.

3. **One qualification, said once.** "endpoint unapproved" now appears only on the
   exercise-boundary line, which is unconditional and sits directly under the
   controls. The field note keeps its own distinct claim — that these are exercise
   times and not wave-arrival margins or advice. The route profile and the route cue
   keep their own facts and lose the repeated qualification.

4. **Dead rules removed.** The eight dead selectors and four keyframe sets are gone.
   Nothing in the stylesheets now names a thing safe, and the blinking "live" dot —
   which had no use and would have implied a live signal that does not exist — is
   gone with it. Motion stays feedback-only.

5. **The legend is a grid.** Four states, two columns when the column is narrow and
   four when it is wide, so the fourth state can never strand itself alone. The
   column count always divides the count.

6. **Every section owns a symbol.** The DDPM risk-evidence page has its own navigation
   symbol and its own current-state marker, and the symbol opens the page.

7. **The caption prints a caption.** The scope qualifier is rendered as an element
   rather than interpolated into a string.

## Verified

Measured on the build that was released, in a real browser.

- **Text bands, 390×844 / 1024×768 / 1440×900:** no stranded word in any band at any
  width; no prose block below 40 characters per line; the document does not scroll
  vertically at any of the three widths; the masthead is 36 px, between 4.0 % and
  4.7 % of the viewport.
- **Water shading:** with a synthetic replay loaded, 62.2 % of the pixels in the
  terrain stage moved between the first and last frame, mean delta RGB
  [29.12, 37.98, 53.24]; the caption read "depth 0.2–0.5 m" at the first frame.
- **Roster:** the four crowd states sum to 1,500 of 1,500 — the population is
  conserved and the counts sit at their model nodes.
- **Drill:** a record entered on the device survives a reload with its counts and its
  route text intact.
- **Research:** all five index links resolve to a visible section.
- **Data and sources:** the dialog opens and stays inside the viewport.
- **Masthead:** one row at 320, 360, 390, 681, 800, 1024 and 1440 px, with no
  horizontal overflow and no page overflow, carrying five section symbols and the
  manual button.
- **Playback:** the exercise clock advances while the task runs and stops when it is
  paused. Note that `advancePlayback` ignores any frame gap over 250 ms by design —
  a rendering stall must not jump the clock ahead — so on a software renderer the
  exercise runs slower than wall time. That is the intended behaviour, not a fault.
- **Suites:** 91 TypeScript tests and 11 Python tests pass; the build is clean; the
  contrast gate passes on all six roles (body 15.44, secondary 6.55, masthead 11.47,
  amber navigation 5.94, action text 8.00, active row 13.72).

## Remaining

Named plainly, because a clean pass may not be claimed while these stand.

1. **Physical-phone testing is outstanding.** Every width here is an emulated
   viewport. Nothing in this record proves touch behaviour, real font rendering or
   thumb reach on a 390×844 device.
2. **The Thai pagination reading test is outstanding.** The Thai strings are shorter
   in characters but not in rendered width; the drill protocol and the research
   dossier have not been read on a phone in Thai.
3. **Roof coverage is still at the source ceiling** — 8 readings against 1,196
   buildings. The Esri z19/z20 imagery over Khao Lak is byte-identical placeholders
   and z18 is 0.59 m per pixel, so the ridge lines are a demonstration, not coverage.
4. **No real research replay ships with the app.** The schema, the import path and
   the synthetic fixtures exist; the published frames do not.
5. **The Rokehan embed still needs one line changed by that tool's author**:
   `frame-ancestors 'self' https://oyasumi-gamedev.com https://tsunami.nonarkara.org`.
   It must not be framed, proxied or header-rewritten from this side.
6. **The viewport and banner receipts are regenerated after the deploy**, so they are
   tied to the bytes the host actually served. A receipt taken against an older
   bundle is not evidence for this one.
