## Interaction and publication contract

- Keep the map as the first visual entry. Its adjacent task column or phone exit carries the warning, next turn and Start/Pause in reading and keyboard order. Keep quantities calibrated with units and intermediate marks. Keep credits, statuses and source actions compact and reachable. Preserve the charts, 1.65 m walk-through, 2D fallback, linework, manual, bilingual navigation and on-device records.
- Keep exercise schedules, illustrative water, imported research frames and published study outcomes distinct in the UI. Only source-supported locations and explicit building heights appear as measured map data. New operational warnings, validated routes and refuge designations are a separate decision with source and validation requirements.
- Use the real browser matrix at 390×844, 1024×768 and 1440×900 plus 320×700, 390×600, 667×375, 1169×674 and 1280×720. Check both sides of the banner breakpoints. Include enlarged Thai and English, keyboard focus, 2D recovery, modal return and a camera-follow interaction. The independent reviewer records geometry defects and their closure.
- Run strict build, tests, contrast, fresh local geometry, live smoke and served-byte verification. Keep each slice independently revertible and within the project's 30% file-shrink rule. Report observed results and unavailable checks in the release notes.

Approval covers these targeted app changes and their commit, push, deployment and live verification. The first approved change is the measured caption and desktop map correction on top of the Phase 1 candidate. Estimate five to seven focused engineering days across the scope. Scheduling a physical device and native readers can run in parallel once the first-screen path is stable.
