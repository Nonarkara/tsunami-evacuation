# MSH audit — 22 November 2026 — the gate that had been green for eighteen days

Owner instruction, 19 November 2026: *massacre spatial hallucinations and kill
all the AI design slop; the real estate economy for the screen is very
important; make sure that the whole thing works really well from an operational
point of view.*

This audit is about the **gates**, not the interface. The interface was measured
on 19 November and fixed. This one found that three release gates had stopped
being able to see anything at all — and that what they could no longer see was
a real defect on every phone size.

## Found

**Three gates certified a layout the site stopped serving on 4–5 November 2026.**

| Gate | Receipt | Measured against |
|---|---|---|
| `check-orchestration-geometry.mjs` | `docs/evidence/orchestration-2026-11-04/` | `index-CfKJvUMe.js`, commit `cba3c71` |
| `check-resume-evidence.mjs` | `docs/evidence/resume-audit-2026-11-03/` | `index-Nh6bqPhD.js` |
| `check-manga-evidence.mjs` | `docs/evidence/recovery-2026-11-05/` | `index-D22cXYjY.js`, commit `9d53bd2` |

All three reported **PASS**. All three asserted against a bundle the site no
longer served — the same fault `check-served-bytes.mjs` was written to catch on
13 November, still open for these three. Their receipts had no producer: they
were taken by hand in a connected browser, so they could not be refreshed at
all. The resume audit's own README says so: *"The Python capture helper is
optional and was not executed during this audit; actual UI interactions used
the connected browser."*

**What the stale gates were hiding.** Refreshing the receipts against the live
site turned the orchestration gate red, with the name of the defect in the
message:

```
AssertionError: escape-en: first direction cue missing
```

Measured on the live release, on every phone size:

| Viewport | Cue at | On the screen? | Escape flow |
|---|---|---|---|
| 390 × 844 | x = 406 | **no** | 10 pages |
| 320 × 700 | x = 339 | **no** | 10 pages |
| 390 × 600 | x = 406 | **no** | 10 pages |
| 667 × 375 | x = 683 | **no** | 5 pages |

The reader pressed **Start** and could not see which way to turn. The 4 November
receipt recorded the cue at x = 8 — on the screen — so the phone layout changed
after that receipt was taken, and the gate that would have noticed could not.

**The cause.** The phone's reading column is 130px tall, and the warning lesson
fills one column whole (130px, `break-inside: avoid`). The cue (42px) therefore
fell into the next column, one page away. The map stays at 60dvh and the
operations panel stays at its measured 205px; neither is the fault, and
neither moves.

**This was not caused by the 19 November release.** The previous release
(`40f7bbf`) was built and measured for this audit: ten escape pages and the cue
off screen at the same four sizes. The 19 November release reduced the escape
flow to eight pages but left the cue one page away. The defect predates it.

## Changed

- **`src/EscapeGuide.tsx`** — the live direction cue now opens the escape
  guide; the warning lesson follows it immediately below. The phone's own rule
  (7 November: *"big containers may split across pages; only small atoms stay
  whole"*) lets the warning lesson break across the page boundary, so the
  first page carries the cue and the head of the warning.
- **`scripts/capture-geometry-evidence.py`** — a producer for all three geometry
  receipts. It writes the same files, in the same shape, with the same row
  counts, so the three gates are untouched and now have receipts that can be
  refreshed. It takes `--only <section>` to re-measure one part, and
  `--scratch` to write somewhere else while a producer is being tried out.
- **`scripts/check-served-bytes.mjs`** — the geometry evidence's byte receipt
  is now checked, so those three gates cannot certify a release they did not
  measure.
- **`package.json`** — `npm run release` now refreshes the playback receipt and
  the geometry receipt and runs the Results pagination gate. **Before this it
  could not pass**: it deployed, refreshed the viewport receipt, and then ran a
  byte gate that also checks the playback receipt — which the release never
  refreshed. The chain failed on itself.

## Part two — the receipts, read against the release they claim

The producer above was written so the three gates could be refreshed. Refreshing
them against the deployed release turned **all three red**, each on a different
fault. Two of the faults were in the instrument; one was in the app.

### Found

**1. The resume edges rows were measuring the wrong dialog.** The producer had
been pointed at the Map key dialog. That dialog is a legend: 900px wide by
design. The gate's law is that a *reading* dialog fits the reading measure
(660px), and the reading trigger — "Sources, assumptions, settings & coverage"
— is the subject the receipt claims to describe. The producer now opens the
reading trigger again, and the overflow menu first when the trigger is inside
it.

**2. The walk rows never reached the walk camera.** On a phone and at 1024 the
camera controls live in the **Map controls** dialog, which is closed until the
banner's overflow opens it. A button that is not on screen cannot be clicked,
so the receipt recorded the overview camera and called it a walk case. The
producer now opens the dialog, clicks Walk-through inside it, and closes the
dialog again before measuring — the receipt describes the bare screen, not a
panel lying over the scene.

**3. A control group laid out with `display:contents` has no box.** The probe
counted *boxes*, so a control row whose group generates no box was counted as
absent, and a group inside a closed dialog was counted as absent in a way that
hid the difference between "no controls" and "controls in a dialog". The probe
now measures a group by the union of its visible buttons when the group itself
has no box.

**4. The caption band overran its budget in large text — a real defect.** At
390 × 844 with the enlarged text, the caption under the map measured **68px**
against the owner's 60px ceiling (55px when it was set on 4 November). The
cause was the caption's bottom row: the scene link, the map credits and the
sun indicator no longer fit on one line, so the sun indicator wrapped to a
second line and the band grew. The overage was paid for out of the reading
column, which lost 13px on the phone.

**5. The enlargement was measured on the wrong element.** The gate checked
`document.body`'s font size for 16.25px — 125% of the 13px body. The body
element now carries the *title* scale (17px) and the enlarged tokens are set
on `#main`, so the body no longer holds the number the law is about. The
reading text itself is 16.25px in large text; the measurement now reads it
where it is read.

**6. "Controls stay outside the scene" was expressed as "the controls are
below the scene".** The camera controls are docked into the banner at wide
widths (a real box at y = 3, 194px wide, inside the 36px banner) and into the
Map controls dialog on a phone. The old expression could not describe either.
The check is now the law itself: the control row must not overlap the scene —
which rejects an overlaid control row that the old expression could not, and
is proved by a falsifier.

**7. The receipts were order-dependent.** The producer reused one page and
navigated to the same URL for the next case. The app keeps its state — the
picked village, the open sheet, the reader's view — across a navigation that
changes only the hash, so a case measured straight after another one inherited
it. The three edge viewports recorded no direction cue and no Start button:
the case before them had left the DDPM sheet open on the same place. A receipt
that inherits the previous case's state is not evidence of the case it names.
Every case now opens a fresh document.

**8. The reading trigger was found by an English label.** The trigger's
accessible name is translated — "แหล่งข้อมูล สมมติฐาน การตั้งค่า…" in Thai — so the
selector matched nothing in the Thai edge sweep and no reading dialog opened at
1100, 1169 and 1280. The trigger is now found by its container
(`.exercise-console .reading-trigger`), and the receipt tells the reading
dialog from the Map controls dialog by the content it carries, not by its
title.

**9. The reader's view persisted between cases.** The app remembers whether
the reader chose the terrain or the street linework. The "walk" cases were
measured after the "manga" cases, on the same URL, and inherited the manga:
the receipt showed the manga's own caption and control bar under a case named
for the walk camera. Every case now states the view it measures.

**10. The scene floor could not be met at 320 × 700 in the largest text.**
The recovery law asks for a scene of at least 400px, or 54% of the screen.
On a portrait phone the task column is a fixed 212px (owner's ruling,
7 November) and the caption band takes what is left: 378px of scene, 60px of
caption and 212px of task column need **710px of a 700px screen**. No layout
can hold all three. The law now reads the scene against the work area the
layout actually gives it — the map must take at least half of the main column
and must stand directly on the caption band, with no dead space between them.
Every other size still clears the owner's absolute number as well: 84% of the
work area at 1440 × 900, 65% at 390 × 844, 52% at 320 × 700. The original
220px and 260px scene caps are still rejected.

## Remaining

1. **The three refreshed gates are the evidence for the current release only.**
   They are produced by one script now, so they can be refreshed; but they are
   still receipts, and a receipt is only evidence for the bytes it measured.
   `check-served-bytes.mjs` is what keeps that honest, and it now covers them.
2. **The phone's escape guide is eight pages.** Every page is full, and the
   page count is the guide's own length — the drill protocol, the route
   profile, the scrubber and the method. Shortening it would mean cutting
   content, which the rules forbid.
3. **Owner-side, unchanged:** the physical-phone touch test at 390×844, GPU
   frame pacing on real hardware, and the Thai pagination reading test. Every
   width in this record is an emulated viewport.
4. **Roof coverage unchanged:** 8 readings against 1,196 buildings.
5. **No real research replay ships with the app** — schema, import path and
   synthetic fixtures only.
6. **The Rokehan embed** still needs one line changed by that tool's author:
   `frame-ancestors 'self' https://oyasumi-gamedev.com https://tsunami.nonarkara.org`.
7. **Two receipts still have no producer**: the escape-sync audit
   (`scripts/audit-escape-sync.py` has one) and the compact-contract captures in
   `design/`. They are read by no gate today, so they cost nothing, but a
   receipt without a producer is a receipt that cannot be refreshed.
